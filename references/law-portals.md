# Where to check the law

Laws on fires, foraging, fishing, hunting, trapping, camping, protected species and protected land vary by country, region, land manager and season. Always check the agency that manages the specific piece of land, on the day.

## United States

- **Fire (national parks):** [NPS — Fire](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/fire/index.htm) Each park’s Superintendent’s Compendium lists local fire and foraging rules.
- **Fire (national forests):** [USFS — Know Before You Go: Fire](https://www.fs.usda.gov/visit/know-before-you-go/fire) Each forest posts Stage 1/2 fire restrictions.
- **Foraging / forest products:** [USFS — Forest products](https://www.fs.usda.gov/visit/know-before-you-go/forest-products)
- **Camping:** [NPS — Camping](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/camping/index.htm)
- **Camping (BLM land):** [BLM — Camping](https://www.blm.gov/programs/recreation/camping)
- **Permits:** [Recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov/)
- **Fishing and hunting:** [Association of Fish & Wildlife Agencies (links to all state agencies)](https://www.fishwildlife.org/)
- **Federal wildlife law:** [US Fish & Wildlife Service](https://www.fws.gov/)

## United Kingdom

- **Access, wild camping, fires (Scotland):** [Scottish Outdoor Access Code](https://www.outdooraccess-scotland.scot/) Statutory access rights; responsible wild camping allowed; local camping-management byelaws apply.
- **Access (England & Wales):** [The Countryside Code](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-countryside-code) No general right to wild camp outside Dartmoor; landowner permission needed.
- **Fishing:** [gov.uk — Fishing licences](https://www.gov.uk/fishing-licences)
- **Foraging law:** [legislation.gov.uk (Theft Act 1968 s.4(3); Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/) Picking for personal use is generally not theft; uprooting wild plants without permission is an offence.

## Canada

- **National parks (fires, camping, fishing permits):** [Parks Canada — Rules and regulations](https://parks.canada.ca/voyage-travel/regles-rules) Crown land, hunting and most fishing rules are provincial.

## Australia

- **Parks (NSW example):** [NSW National Parks](https://www.nationalparks.nsw.gov.au/) Total Fire Ban days are declared by state fire services.
- **Parks (Victoria example):** [Parks Victoria](https://www.parks.vic.gov.au/)

## New Zealand

- **Fires, huts, camping, fishing, hunting:** [Department of Conservation](https://www.doc.govt.nz/)

## Sweden

- **Right of public access:** [Naturvårdsverket — Allemansrätten](https://www.naturvardsverket.se/en/topics/the-right-of-public-access/) Broad camping and foraging rights; fires restricted by county bans.

## Finland

- **Everyman’s right:** [Metsähallitus — Everyman’s right](https://www.nationalparks.fi/everymansright) Fires need the landowner’s permission.

## Norway

- **Right of access:** [Norwegian Environment Agency — Allemannsretten](https://www.miljodirektoratet.no/) General ban on fires in or near forest 15 April – 15 September.

## Israel

- **Nature reserves & national parks:** [Israel Nature and Parks Authority](https://en.parks.org.il/) Overnight stays only at designated campgrounds; fires only where designated; protected plants may not be picked.
- **Forests and forest campsites:** [KKL-JNF](https://www.kkl.org.il/eng/) Seasonal fire rules.

## International

- **Ethics:** [Leave No Trace — Seven Principles](https://lnt.org/why/7-principles/)
- **Beacon registration:** [Cospas-Sarsat country registries](https://www.cospas-sarsat.int/en/) Register a PLB in its country of registration.
