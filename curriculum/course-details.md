# Course details (for Tal's Academy, not imported)

- Slug: `survival`. Repo: `tal-giladi/survival-course` (public).
- Free or paid: paid (suggestion).
- Risk notice: Wilderness skills involve real risk: cold, heat, water, fire, tools, terrain and wildlife. This course
  does not replace hands-on training (wilderness first aid, navigation, bushcraft); follow the safety class on every
  exercise and check local laws on fires, foraging, fishing and camping.
- Summary: Wilderness survival, bushcraft, first aid and emergency preparedness taught as one decision system:
  Observe, Assess, Prioritize, Plan, Act, Reassess.
- Who it is for: hikers, travellers, outdoor learners and preppers who want judgment, not a list of tricks.
- Format: 135 lessons in 19 stages with diagrams, safety-classified practical exercises, 48 interactive simulations and
  branching scenarios, a multiple-choice quiz per lesson and per stage, 12 capstone scenarios and a final assessment.
- Prerequisites: none.
- What you will learn:
  - Set survival priorities and pick the next highest-value action under stress.
  - Manage heat, water, shelter and fire in forest, desert, mountain, tropical, arctic, coastal and urban settings.
  - Navigate with map, compass, sun, stars and terrain, and recover when navigation fails.
  - Assess and treat injuries with a wilderness first-aid system until help arrives.
  - Read weather and environmental hazards, track, use rope and improvise equipment.
  - Signal for rescue and understand how searches work.
  - Plan and run multi-day survival, alone or with a group.
